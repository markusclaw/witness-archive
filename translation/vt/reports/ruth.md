- back-translation: _Naomi took the child, put him in her lap, and took charge of raising him._
- **info** · benchmark: "se encargó de criarlo" (took charge of raising him) is more specific than the Hebrew אֹמֶנֶת which means "nurse" or "caretaker." The BSB has "became a nurse to him," which is closer to the Hebrew sense of nursing/caring without specifying raising or upbringing as the primary focus.
